Word wrap only breaks before glyphs the shaper marked safe to break

Each glyph is drawn at the x/y glyph reports. Red glyphs carry HarfRust's UNSAFE_TO_BREAK flag. The font (Fredoka-Regular, OFL) has GPOS kerning between letters and the space, so the first glyph of most words is unsafe, and glyph never breaks a line before an unsafe glyph. With "kern off on space characters" the kern feature is disabled only on the spaces, which restores the breaks.
